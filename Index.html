BoodooScan Auto Guide

Batch queue workflow, per-tube notes, Research Mode, and photo tips

Step 1

Load One or Multiple Photos

Select multiple rack photos at once with Upload Photo(s), drag & drop a folder of photos, tap Take Photo on mobile, or press Ctrl+V / Cmd+V to paste from your clipboard.

Step 2

Step Through & Frame Liquid Tips

Click thumbnails in the Batch Queue (or press ← / → arrow keys) to review each photo. Use Apply Box to All if your photos share the same rack framing.

Step 3

Add Per-Photo & Per-Tube Notes

Each photo remembers its own Photo Notes, Roles (C / NT / S), tube names, and individual Tube Notes (e.g., 10^4, Milk, Rep 1) as you click through your batch.

Step 4

Save One or Save All → Export CSV

Click Save Run for the active photo or Save All to log every queued photo into the auto-saved Session Log, then click Export CSV.

Quick Shortcuts:

• Navigate Batch: Press ← or → arrow keys (when not typing in a text box) to jump between photos.

• Paste Image: Copy any photo from your browser/computer and press Ctrl+V (or Cmd+V on Mac) to append it to the queue.

By default, users see Tube, Tube Note, Role, Signal, and strict binary Result (POSITIVE or NEGATIVE). Toggle Research Mode on to inspect all metrics below (all 24 columns are always exported to CSV).

Signal (Rel A_G/A_R)

Beer–Lambert pseudo-absorbance ratio (AG/AR) normalized to the Water Control (= 1.00). Mirrors spectrophotometer A520/A620. Default cutoff: ≥ 1.125 for 10-minute photos, the mean CSFV value relative to water in the specificity photos (or calibrated to Non-Target tubes).

Rel R/G

Direct red-to-green ratio (R/G) normalized to the Water Control's R/G (= 1.00) on BoodooScan's glare-trimmed pixels—no exponential stretching and independent of white-card placement.

Orig App (a·eᵃ)

Formula used for the Bio-Analytics values in the dissertation, calculated from RGB averages: a = R/G, b = a × exp(a), normalized to the Water Control (bsample / bcontrol) on the exact same pixels.

a* (CIE Redness)

Green-to-red color axis in CIE L*a*b* space. Used for the QC Gate: fully aggregated controls (a* ≤ 4.5) pass, partly aggregated controls (a* 4.5 to 24) show WARN, and red/pink controls (a* > 24) flag INVALID.

Δa* & ΔE

Δa* is redness shift vs. Water Control (a*sample − a*water). ΔE is total 3D Euclidean color distance across L*, a*, and b*.

Sig / Max NT

Sample Signal divided by the highest Non-Target (NT) tube in the same photo. Automatically activates when any tube is tagged Non-Target (NT).

  • 1 Keep the Scan Box below the upper plastic lip: Frame just the liquid tips and the white rack background around them so shadows on the upper tube barrels don't pull the rings upward.
  • 2 Always include the Water Control (C) in the same photo: Keeping the negative control and samples in one horizontal row under identical lighting eliminates camera auto-exposure drift.
  • 3 Avoid direct flash or harsh overhead glare: Diffused room lighting produces the cleanest, most repeatable measurements.
  • 4 Keep tape below the liquid tips: Place colored labeling tape below the conical tube tips so the cyan Scan Box can frame the liquid without overlapping the tape.
