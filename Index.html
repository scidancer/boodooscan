BoodooScan Auto — Guide & Metrics

1. Quick Workflow & Keyboard Shortcuts

  • Upload multiple photos (including iPhone .HEIC), drag-and-drop, or paste with Ctrl+V.
  • Use the Inline Photo Switcher right above the results table (or ← / → arrow keys) to move between photos.
  • While typing in Tube Note or Tube boxes, press ↓ or ↑ to jump straight to the next or previous tube row.
  • Use Save & Next → to log the current photo and advance immediately.

2. Water-Control QC Gate

Evaluates the assigned Water (C) tube using CIE a* (red–green axis): red/pink controls (a* > 24) flag INVALID, partly aggregated controls (a* 4.5 to 24) show WARN (calls still displayed), and fully purple controls (a* ≤ 4.5) show PASS.

3. Metrics & Formulas (Research Mode & CSV)

  • Rel AG/AR (Signal): White-balanced Beer–Lambert green-to-red pseudo-absorbance ratio (A_G / A_R) normalized to Water Control. Default cutoff: ≥ 1.125 for 10-minute photos (mean CSFV relative to water in specificity photos) or calibrated to Non-Target tubes.
  • Rel R/G: Plain red-to-green ratio (R / G) normalized to Water Control using glare-trimmed pixels (middle 70% by luminance).
  • Orig App (Orig_BioAnalytics_SN): Formula used for the Bio-Analytics values in the dissertation, calculated from the Bio-Analytics app's RGB averages: a = R/G, b = a × exp(a), S/N = b_sample / b_control.
  • App 5px (App_Export_SN_5px): Realistic simulation of the original Bio-Analytics Android app export: averages rPoint = (R+G+B)/R over 5 random unfiltered pixels inside each ring, computes exp(-rPoint), and divides by Water Control.
  • App Area (App_Export_SN_Area): Applies the exact same exp(-(R+G+B)/R) formula to BoodooScan's glare-trimmed tube area for direct 5-pixel vs. full-area comparison.
  • CIE a*, Δa*, ΔE: Perceptually uniform color space metrics relative to the Water Control.
