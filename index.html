BoodooScan Auto Guide

Quick workflow, Research Mode metrics, and photo tips

Step 1

Take or Drop a Photo

Tap Take Photo on Android or drag and drop a rack image directly onto the dark canvas. Everything runs locally in your browser—no photos leave your device.

Step 2

Frame the Liquid Tips

Drag any of the 4 edges of the cyan Scan Box around the white rack and liquid tips to crop out pipette boxes, caps, and yellow tape. Auto-detection re-runs when you release.

Step 3

Check Roles (C / NT / S)

The leftmost tube defaults to Water Control (C). You can change any tube to Non-Target (NT) (e.g., CSFV or bacteria) or Sample (S), rename labels inline, or drag circles to nudge.

Step 4

Check QC, Save & Export

Verify the Water-Control QC Gate shows PASS, click Save Run (auto-saved in your browser), and click Export CSV when finished with your batch.

Tip for very pale / heavily aggregated tubes: Lower the Sensitivity slider slightly or click + Add Manual Tube to place a sampling ring directly on the liquid.

By default, everyday users see only Tube, Role, Signal, and Result. Toggle Research Mode on to inspect every colorimetric metric below (all 21 columns are always included in the exported CSV).

Signal (Rel A_G/A_R)

Beer–Lambert pseudo-absorbance ratio (green channel optical density divided by red channel optical density), normalized to the Water Control (= 1.00). Mirrors a spectrophotometer's A520/A620 ratio; positive red tubes are typically ≥ 1.30.

a* (CIE Redness)

Green-to-red color axis in CIE L*a*b* space, separated from brightness. Combined with the blue-yellow axis (b*) and R/B ratio for the QC Gate: purple/violet/gray controls pass, while unaggregated red/pink controls (a* > 24, b* > -6, R/B > 1.20) are flagged INVALID.

Δa*

Redness shift compared to the Water Control (a*sample − a*water). Near 0 means it matches the negative control; ≥ 10.0 indicates target DNA kept the GNPs red.

ΔE

Total 3D Euclidean color distance from the Water Control across L*, a*, and b*. Values < 3 look nearly identical to the human eye; values > 10 are clearly distinct.

Legacy R/B

Simple linear raw Red/Blue ratio normalized to the Water Control, included so you can benchmark the new CIE L*a*b* and Beer–Lambert metrics against the original app formula.

Sig / Max NT

Sample Signal divided by the highest Non-Target (NT) tube in the same photo. Automatically activates when any tube is tagged Non-Target (NT).

  • 1 Use a matte white backdrop: Place the 0.2 mL PCR tubes in a white 3D-printed rack or against clean white paper so the detector can locate the white-balance patch (W) and separate pale aggregated tubes cleanly.
  • 2 Always include the Water Control (C) in the same photo: Keeping the negative control and samples in one horizontal row under identical lighting eliminates camera auto-exposure drift.
  • 3 Avoid direct flash or harsh overhead glare: Even though BoodooScan trims the brightest 15% and darkest 15% of pixels inside each tube, diffused room lighting produces the most repeatable measurements.
  • 4 Keep tape below the liquid tips: Place colored labeling tape below the conical tube tips so the cyan Scan Box can frame the liquid without overlapping the tape.
